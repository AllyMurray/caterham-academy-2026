Step 1 of 5

Driver Fit

Start with helmet clearance. The generated checklist will only show the path that matches your measurement.

Helmet clearance

Measure with the driver belted in, helmet and FHR on, in the normal driving position.

Seat plan

With 5 cm or more clearance, choose whether to keep the original Tillett seat or fit a bead seat by preference.

Spacer adjustment

Fit spacers under the front of the seat to tilt it back and lower the driver's helmet, then recheck clearance.

Seat plan after spacers

With clearance restored to 5 cm or more, choose whether to keep the Tillett seat or move to a bead seat.
